## Definitions

- **CSAT%** — CSAT_ANSWER_CODE 1-3 - extremely satisfied / very satisfied / satisfied. Neutral (4) counts as NOT satisfied. The underlying scale is inverted: 1 is best.
- **CES%** — CSAT_EFFORT_CODE 1-2 - strongly agree / agree that the request was easy to handle. Higher is better, so this is the inverse of the '% not easy' figure used elsewhere in this analysis.
- **ASAT%** — AGENT_CSAT_CODE 1-2 - strongly agree / agree on a separate agent-directed question. Answered on only 2,306 tickets corpus-wide, the thinnest of the three.
- **Suppression** — A percentage is blank where fewer than 30 customers answered that question for that rule. The count is always shown.
- **rejection_reasons_10pct_plus** — Every rejection reason accounting for at least 10% of the rule's design-weighted volume, up to three. Reason and rule are a MANY-TO-MANY mapping - a rule averages 8.4 distinct reasons - so n_distinct_reasons is given alongside.
- **resolution_code_designated** — The primary code the triangulation matrix assigns to the rule. Where it differs from most_logged the rule cannot conform, however well it is applied.
- **code_vol_2025** — CODE-LEVEL, not rule-level, same as in csd_2026_rule_scorecard.xlsx: the full-year 2025 volume of this rule's designated resolution code, from 'resolution codes osvc-Copy.xlsx'. Several rules share one primary code, so this number repeats across those rows and cannot be summed to a rule-level total.
- **Basis** — 12,000 stratified sample; est_population is design-weighted to the 50,714 classifiable tickets closed Jan-Jul 2026 (seven months, not a year).
