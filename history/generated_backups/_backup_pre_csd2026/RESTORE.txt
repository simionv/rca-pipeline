This notebook (08_csd_2026_validation_rules.ipynb) replaced the ScienceDirect 2026
modules in generated/pipeline_config.json with the CSD 2026 ones.

To put ScienceDirect back:

    python build_pipeline.py build --input definitions/sd_2026_pipeline_definition.xlsx --force-all

The ScienceDirect outputs are already saved under runs/output/sd_2026_*; nothing
was deleted. To come back to CSD afterwards, rebuild from
definitions/csd_2026_pipeline_definition.xlsx the same way.
